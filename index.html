Pick any two sheets

The printed family kit splits your key into three sheets. Keep one at home, give one to your sister, put one in a drawer at work. This is the same two-of-three scheme the app uses, running in your browser on a sample secret.

The vault is shutChoose two sheets. Without the script, the idea is the same: any two of the three printed sheets rebuild the key; one sheet alone is just noise.

Straight answers

Can you unlock my vault if I forget everything? No. We never hold your keys, so there is nothing for us to reset. That keeps everyone else out, and it means a lost phrase cannot be replaced by us. This is why recovery is proven before your first save.

What happens on a new iPhone? Sign in with the same Apple Account with iCloud Keychain on. The key arrives through iCloud Keychain and the files through iCloud. If the key has not arrived yet, the app says it is waiting instead of showing an empty vault. Your 12 words open it either way.

Where are my files stored? On your devices and in your own iCloud, encrypted before they leave the device. Apple stores the encrypted data; neither Apple nor we can read it. We run no server that receives your documents.

What if I stop paying? You keep everything and can read, open and export every file. You only lose the ability to add beyond the free limits and the family features. Lifetime stays lifetime.

How does a trusted person get in? They ask from their own iPhone. Nothing opens until the waiting period you chose has passed and you approve. You can refuse at any time before that.

I lost the paper with my words. While you can still open the app, make a new phrase in Settings. If the paper may have been seen by someone else, re-encrypt the vault too, so the old words open nothing.

Do you track how I use the app? No analytics by default and no ads. Crash reports reach us only if you share them with developers in your iPhone settings.

When can I use it? Lockhearth is in testing now and comes to the App Store in early 2027, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch at the same time.
